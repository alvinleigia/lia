Conversation Quality

Open a project, then Automation > Conversation Quality.

1. Open Needs review, or use the 10% sample to inspect ordinary conversations.
2. Read the redacted transcript and recorded processing evidence.
3. Select a bot reply and mark Correct, Wrong answer, Unnecessary detail or
   Missed intent. Add a short note describing the expected behaviour.
4. Optional: Prepare AI review draft for a flagged or sampled conversation.
   This makes one metered model call; its suggestions need human review.
5. Turn a finding into a synthetic regression case. Test the proposed knowledge
   or workflow correction before publishing it.

Rules refresh on page load, across the latest 50 conversations and their latest
100 messages. They flag possible fallbacks, repeated questions, slow replies,
failed actions and active enquiries idle for 24 hours. They do not prove an
error or automatically fix anything. Expected FAQ interruptions can be valid.

The detailed transcript contains up to 200 recent messages. AI review receives
up to 30 redacted messages. Redaction reduces exposure but is not a guarantee
that every personal detail is detected; do not enter personal data in notes.

New structured channel turns record stage, processing duration, attempts,
tokens and grounding reference IDs. These records do not contain prompts,
field values or private reasoning. document IDs reference retrieved chunks;
project_approved_answer indexes are local to the turn, not durable source IDs.
Historical usage may be unavailable. Token counts are not dollar costs.

No automatic scheduler or live knowledge/workflow editing is enabled.
Review data stays scoped to its project.
