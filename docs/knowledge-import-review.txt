KNOWLEDGE IMPORT REVIEW

For any business domain:
1. Open Project > Documents and upload PDF, Markdown or text.
2. Review flagged wording, uncertain details, test labels and claims.
3. Edit the draft, or use optional AI wording suggestions, then save.
4. Compare against the original and verify the business facts.
5. Approve the saved draft, then process approved documents.

Nothing new enters chat before approval and successful indexing. Editing or a
failed indexing attempt keeps the previous published content available.

Use Q: Question A: Answer or Question => Answer for explicit FAQ answers.
Published FAQs belong to their document, not project-specific runtime code.
Exact questions and supported discourse prefixes reuse them without an LLM.
Paraphrases and other questions continue through the shared knowledge runtime.
Deleting the source removes its imported FAQ answers and indexed content.

Rules flag issues; they do not prove every statement is true or find every
semantic contradiction. Conflicting answers to the same normalized question
and source-report wording block publication. Owners must verify other flags.
AI suggestions process at most 12 flagged lines per request, with recorded usage.
They are never automatically approved and must not turn unknowns into promises.

Older documents remain available. "Review knowledge" prepares a draft from
their indexed text when no original was retained; replace overlapping passages
with the original document text before approval where possible.
