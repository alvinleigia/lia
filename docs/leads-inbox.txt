LEADS INBOX

Open Projects > Leads Inbox (/projects/leads).
The inbox spans projects in the selected company. Filter by project, lead status,
channel, or search collected details. Open a record to see all collected fields,
the linked conversation, ownership, notes, and the next follow-up date.

Saved submissions and requests sent for team review appear automatically.
Incomplete sessions and requests cancelled before submission are excluded.
There is one inbox record per submission; no copying or extra LLM call is needed.

Fields come from the pinned published workflow and AI task versions, with stable
keys and native values. Old, unversioned submissions retain their original keys.
Changing the current workflow does not relabel historical records.

Lead status (New, Assigned, Contacted, Converted, Closed) is separate from workflow
execution status. Assignment requires an active company member. Updates use
revision checks and save notes plus audit events atomically.
Follow-up dates are tracking fields; saving one does not schedule a call or message.

Authenticated, tenant-scoped JSON reads:
GET /api/leads?projectId=123&status=new&page=1
GET /api/leads/456
Detail JSON includes fields and fieldDefinitions (key, label, type, required).
Existing project export settings apply. These endpoints use the signed-in session;
external CRM authentication and delivery must be configured separately.

Existing contacts remain linked to their conversations. This release does not
merge people automatically across projects or convert ordinary FAQ chats to leads.
