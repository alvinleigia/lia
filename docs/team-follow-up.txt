Team follow-up during an enquiry

In Business Task > Behavior > Team follow-up, map four existing fields:
- Consent: an optional Yes/No field.
- Question for the team: an optional text field.
- Visitor name: a required text field.
- Contact: a required phone or email field.

Connect the completed task result directly to a Human Handoff step, then publish
the task and the flow. Without this destination, Lia does not offer follow-up.

When an answer is unavailable or refers a question to the team, Lia retains the
question and asks whether the visitor wants follow-up. Yes/No is handled locally.
Yes requests missing name/contact details with their purpose; known details and
the active enquiry remain intact. No resumes the enquiry without requesting a
callback. Ordinary FAQ answers retain their normal collection behavior.

This collects a follow-up request as part of the current enquiry. Required enquiry
fields and final confirmation still apply. The question and consent reach the
existing team queue after submission. It does not promise a call, notify a team
without a configured notification, or mark a booking confirmed.

UAT: unanswered question -> Yes -> contact details -> Confirm -> inspect team
queue. Repeat with No, a FAQ interruption, and Cancel. Check that a Yes to the
follow-up offer cannot submit a request, even when all other fields are present.
